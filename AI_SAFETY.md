# AI Safety

## Principles

1. **Never invent facts** - AI must not fabricate information
2. **Never invent laws** - AI must not cite non-existent legislation
3. **Never invent contacts** - AI must not fabricate government phone numbers or URLs
4. **Never accuse** - AI must not declare guilt or corruption
5. **Never guarantee resolution** - AI must not promise outcomes
6. **Distinguish user claims from verified facts** - Always label appropriately
7. **Ask clarification when uncertain** - Don't guess
8. **Escalate urgent situations** - Prioritize safety

## What AI CAN Do

- Understand natural language input
- Classify into predefined scenarios
- Detect relevant entities
- Translate between languages
- Summarize user input
- Ask conversational clarification questions
- Find similar reported scenarios

## What AI MUST NOT Do

- Declare someone guilty
- Declare a police officer corrupt
- Provide unsupported legal conclusions
- Fabricate government contacts
- Fabricate laws
- Fabricate complaint procedures
- Invent evidence
- Automatically mark a report as valid
- Automatically mark a report as resolved

## Scenario Matching

When AI classifies an incident, it returns:
- Scenario name
- Confidence score
- Reason

This is labeled as "Scenario similarity" NOT "Legal classification" or "Official determination."

## Fallback

If AI is unavailable, the application continues to work with deterministic workflows. Users can still:
- Select categories manually
- Fill structured forms
- Submit incidents
- Track reports
